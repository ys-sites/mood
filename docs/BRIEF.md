# CAMPAIGN BRIEF: CHRISTMAS 2026 "BUY ONE. GIVE ONE."
**Brand**: Shawarma Moods, Montréal  
**Tagline**: Spread the Mood.  
**Target Window**: Christmas 2026 (Distribution Target: Dec 23, 2026)  
**Status**: Private Owner-Facing Marketing & Operations Proposal (Conceptual)

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Shawarma Moods proposes a landmark holiday campaign for Christmas 2026: **Buy One. Give One. (Spread the Mood)**.
Unlike typical promotional discounts, this initiative leverages community generosity during the Montréal winter holiday season. A customer purchases two shawarmas—keeping one fresh meal for themselves and funding one hot shawarma to be prepared and delivered to someone in need.

To demonstrate shared commitment, Shawarma Moods pledges an additional **$1 restaurant contribution** per verified donated meal directly into a dedicated community kitchen fund.

**Initial Campaign Milestone**: 1,000 Donated Shawarmas ($1,000 Restaurant Contribution).

---

## 2. Core Narrative Architecture (Strict Order)
1. **The Emotional Hook**: Winter in Montréal; the warmth of a community gathering over food; turning holiday dining into a dignified act of generosity.
2. **The Mechanism**: Transparent 3-step customer-funded structure (Buy 2, Keep 1, Donate 1).
3. **The Shared Commitment**: Shawarma Moods matches with $1 per meal.
4. **The 1,000 Meal Challenge**: Transparent live tracking, clear milestone tiers.
5. **Community Delivery**: Dec 23 distribution with vetted Montréal shelter/community partners; high food-safety and dignified protocol.
6. **Creator Amplification**: Authentic local food creators and micro-influencers; zero misleading viral promises.
7. **B2B Business Expansion**: Model demonstrated through Kin Academy case study (e.g., 1 enrollment = 1 meal funded).
8. **Commercial Supporting Offers**: $10 promotional shawarma, student combo, 10-person Family Holiday Box.
9. **Financial & Operational Feasibility**: Transparent unit economics, margin resilience, risk mitigation.
