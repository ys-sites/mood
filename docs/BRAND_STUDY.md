# Shawarma Moods — Instagram Brand Study

Source: 6 owner-supplied screenshots of the @shawarmamoods grid (~48 posts) plus the public profile header.
The profile itself is login-gated, so captions, hashtags, highlights and post dates could not be read.
Colors below were measured from screenshot pixels (median of in-region pixels), not eyeballed.

## 1. Account facts (public header, read 2026-11-15)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Handle | @shawarmamoods — verified |
| Followers / following | 19.6K / 715 |
| Bio line 1 | **"ES-TU DANS LE MOOD?"** |
| Footprint | **8 locations, QC & ON** — Laval, Montréal, Saint-Jérôme, Oakville; Centre-Ville de Montréal flagged |

**Implication for the pitch:** this is a multi-location chain, not one restaurant. The site currently says `location: "Montréal, QC"` and talks about "the kitchen". Before building further, decide whether the campaign is one flagship (Centre-Ville) or chain-wide. Chain-wide, 1,000 meals is ~125 per location. One location, it is 1,000 meals from a single kitchen on Dec 23. Approval may also need franchise or head-office sign-off.

**Gap in the current site:** the code uses Tailwind `amber-500 #F59E0B` / `amber-400 #FBBF24` (~300 usages). Amber-500 is more orange and more saturated than the brand gold. Recommend `--brand-gold: #EAAA2F` as the main accent and `#FBCE22` only for price and offer badges.

## 3. Typography — four voices

1. **Tall condensed caps** — "YOUR DIET TEXTED.", "CRISPY", "SHAWARMA MOODS IN TOWN", "BECAUSE ONE IS NEVER ENOUGH". Bebas Neue / Oswald / Anton territory. *Site already uses Bebas Neue ✔*
2. **Wide heavy geometric caps** — "CRISPY MOODS SHAWARMA" on the anniversary poster, "DRIPPIN GOOD", "SATISFY". Montserrat ExtraBold/Black feel. *Missing from the site*: add Montserrat 800/900 for promo-poster-style blocks.
3. **Light/regular sans captions** — "Crispy Moods 1 year anniversary", "Send this to your friend…". Montserrat/Poppins regular, white with a soft shadow on photos.
4. **Handwritten script** — "Soft Bun → Juicy Chicken → Garlic → Fries" with drawn arrows, plus the logo's "Shawarma" script. Used for playful annotation, never for headlines.

**Headline pattern:** one big gold line plus one smaller white line, with a single word bolded for the punch.
`YOUR DIET TEXTED.` (gold, condensed) / `WE'LL ANSWER **LATER**.` (white, smaller)

## 4. Language

- **French-first, bilingual.** Promo posters stack FR large over EN small ("CÉLÈBRE SON PREMIER ANNIVERSAIRE / CELEBRATES ITS FIRST ANNIVERSARY"; "TOUT LE MOIS DE NOVEMBRE / ALL NOVEMBER LONG").
- Casual posts switch freely: "À quoi ressemble vraiment cet endroit !", "Quand le shish taouk rencontre la baguette", "Change de mood, prends un shawarma".
- **"MOOD" stays in English inside French sentences.** That is the brand's own franglais hook.
- Price format on posters: `9.99$` (dollar after).

**Implication:** the French tagline should follow the house pattern. **"PARTAGE LE MOOD"** fits next to "Es-tu dans le MOOD?" better than a literal translation. Campaign poster mockups should be FR-over-EN, which also fits Québec signage norms.

## 5. Copy voice

Meme-literate, relationship/texting humor, share-bait, food-pleasure first. Never preachy.

| Observed line | Device |
|---|---|
| "Send this to your friend — no reply in 5 seconds, they owe you a Crispy Moods Shawarma!" | DM-share challenge |
| "Send this to your shawarma partner" | Tag/share bait |
| "Your diet texted. We'll answer later." | Personification joke |
| "**Because one is never enough**" | Excess/abundance |
| "Sooooo DRIPPIN GOOD", "Satisfy your cravings" | Sensory hype |
| Pantone card: "FRESH, JUICY, IRRESISTIBLE" | Design-culture parody |
| Fake Instagram post frame around a wrap | Platform parody |
| "THE SHAWARMA LINE-UP!", "SHAWARMA MOODS IN TOWN ⚽" | Sports/event tie-ins |

**Direct hooks for BUY ONE. GIVE ONE.** (copy drafts, not yet approved):
- *"Because one is never enough."* The brand already said it, and buying two now has a reason.
- *"Send this to your shawarma partner"* becomes *"Tag who you're buying two with."* It reuses an existing share mechanic without promising virality.
- *"Es-tu dans le MOOD?"* is answered by *"Partage le MOOD."*

## 6. Content pillars (share of the ~48 posts, approx.)

1. **Food macro hero (~45%)**: crisp grill-marked wraps, cross-section stacks, sauce and pomegranate-molasses drips, flat-lays, sharing platters with fries.
2. **Process / hands (~20%)**: black nitrile gloves, flaming rotisserie, carving the cone, squeeze-bottle drizzle, pan-frying.
3. **Owner as the face (~15%)**: bald, bearded owner in a black logo tee and gold chain. Seen on the phone ("À quoi ressemble vraiment cet endroit !"), on lav-mic talking-head reels, under the neon "Es-tu dans le MOOD?", in front of the "Habibi" Lebanese-heritage wall.
4. **Community and events (~10%)**: grand-opening red carpet with staff and black balloons, aerial shot of a tent event with a crowd, partner/visitor photo, creator street-interview reel ("THIS IS SHAWARMA MOODS AND THEY ARE…"), soccer tie-in.
5. **Promo posters (~5%)**: 1-year anniversary, 9.99$ Crispy Moods "all November".
6. **Menu range**: bowls, mixed plates, falafel bowl, shish taouk baguette, sub sandwich.

## 7. Recurring physical brand assets

- Black/white **checkered wrapper** with a round black-and-gold logo sticker (the most recognizable prop)
- Black kraft **bag**, gold logo, FB/IG/TikTok icons; black bowls printed "Follow & Tag Us"
- Wooden **Shawarma Moods–branded serving boards**
- Black interior, **yellow neon** "MOOD" signage, Lebanese decor (cedar, "Liban" stamp, coffee pot, "Habibi")
- Staff in black logo tees and black caps

## 8. What this means for the pitch site

| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Switch accent to `#EAAA2F`; keep `#FBCE22` for offer badges | Orange-leaning amber as the main gold |
| Add a wide heavy sans (Montserrat 800/900) for poster-style blocks | Only-Bebas headline monotony |
| Gold headline + white sub-line + one bolded word | Long, earnest charity paragraphs |
| FR-first bilingual mockups; "Partage le MOOD" | Literal "Répandez l'humeur" style translations |
| Owner as narrator of the 60s/5-min video, which matches his existing reels | Anonymous voice-over or stock families |
| Checkered wrapper, gloves, neon and fire as the festive cues; add warm string lights only | Red-green template Christmas |
| Humor in the hook, sincerity in the mechanism and figures | Jokes about the people receiving meals |

## 9. Open questions for the owner

1. **Scope:** one location (which one?) or all 8? Does head office or the franchisor approve?
2. **Promo price:** the grid shows **9.99$ "tout le mois de novembre"** for the anniversary. Does it continue into December? The site currently shows "$10 Holiday Solo Deal, normally $15", which is unconfirmed.
3. **Owner on camera:** is the owner willing to front the campaign video, as in existing reels?
4. **Assets:** can we get the original logo file and raw food photos? The screenshots are low-res and must not be used as final site imagery.
